# Why RAG?

**LLM alone**

```text
Question → LLM → Answer
```

**RAG**

```text
Question
   ↓
Retrieve knowledge
   ↓
LLM + retrieved context
   ↓
Grounded answer
```

The model has **general knowledge**.

Our club documents contain **specific knowledge**.

**RAG connects the two.**
