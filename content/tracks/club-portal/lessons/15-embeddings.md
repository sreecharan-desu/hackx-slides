# Embeddings

**Embedding == numerical representation of meaning**

```text
"How do I join the AWS club?"
              ↓
        [0.12, -0.31, ...]
```

Similar meanings produce vectors that are **close in vector space**.

```text
Question vector
      ↓
Vector similarity
      ↓
Relevant chunks
```
