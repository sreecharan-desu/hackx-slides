# Why Chunk Documents?

**Don't send the entire document.**

Instead:

```text
Document
 ↓
Chunk 1
Chunk 2
Chunk 3
Chunk 4
...
```

If a user asks about workshop timings, we don't need to send the entire 20-page document to the model.

### Benefits

- Relevant context
- Lower token usage
- Better retrieval
- Better response quality
